About the map

Edition eleven is drawn entirely as pixel art: every building, tree and street, pixel by pixel. The map covers every district of Tashkent, including Chilonzor, Yunusobod, Sergeli, Yangihayot, Bektemir and New Tashkent. Search for a district or click its label on the map. Coverage, city and surroundings: —. You can also search — metro stations. Zoom in for latticed facades, balconies, glass towers and blue domes. The time of day, weather and season follow real Tashkent unless you choose otherwise. Geography comes from OpenStreetMap; mapped heights and roof shapes are used where they exist. Missing heights, facades, trees and colours are an artistic interpretation.
